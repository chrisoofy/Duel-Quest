🧭 Quick Directions

  1. Choose a game mode, then press Start / Restart Game.
  2. On your turn, press Roll Dice & Move once. Your token moves 1–6 spaces.
  3. 🪙 Coin spaces award coins; 🤝 Partner spaces recruit an ally (maximum 3); ⚔️ Duel spaces award 2 coins.
  4. If both leaders land on the same space, use Attack or Defend when it is your battle turn.
  5. Allies protect the leader by taking damage first. Defeat the rival leader to win.
  6. Press Start / Restart Game at any time to reset the board, health, coins, allies, and log.

Only the current player can act. Actions are disabled during movement, during the other fighter's turn, and after the game ends.

📜 Duel Quest Rules

Goal: Defeat your rival in a duel to win the game.

  1. Choose a mode: Play against the CPU or with two local players, then select Start Game.
  2. Take turns: On your turn, select Roll Dice & Move. You move forward 1–6 spaces around the board.
  3. 🪙 Coin spaces: Collect 3–6 coins.
  4. 🤝 Partner spaces: Recruit a random ally, up to 3 allies. If your team is full, receive 5 coins instead.
  5. ⚔️ Duel spaces: Receive 2 coins.
  6. Meet your rival: If both players land on the same space, a duel begins.
  7. Battle actions: The active fighter chooses Attack to deal damage or Defend to reduce the next incoming hit. Turns alternate between fighters.
  8. Allies in battle: Your team’s attack power is added to your leader’s attack. Incoming damage hits allies first; defeated allies are removed from your team.
  9. Win: Reduce your rival leader’s HP to 0. Start a new game to play again.

Tip: Check Team Status to see your HP, coins, attack power, and recruited partners.
